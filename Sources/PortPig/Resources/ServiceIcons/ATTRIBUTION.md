# Service icon attribution

Most brand icons are exported from the
[SVG Logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) Iconify collection through
`@iconify-json/logos@1.2.13`. SVG Logos is released under CC0 1.0.

The `astro.svg`, `caddy.svg`, `elixir.svg`, `rails.svg`, and `rust.svg` assets are
sourced from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) because SVG Logos doesn't currently
provide suitable equivalents for a 22-point row icon. Simple Icons is released
under CC0 1.0.

Individual icons may be subject to their respective owners' trademark rights
and brand guidelines. They are used here only to identify the corresponding
local process or service.
